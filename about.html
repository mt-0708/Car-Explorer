Project Summary

What Makes a Car Worth Its Price? investigates how vehicle sticker price (MSRP), engine horsepower, fuel economy, electric driving range, and manufacturer positioning relate to one another—combining verified MSRP benchmarks with recent 2022–2027 vehicle specifications and 50 years (1975–2025) of U.S. EPA fleet trends.

Author & Course Information

  • Author: Mayah Torres (@mt-0708)
  • Project GitHub Repository: https://github.com/mt-0708/Car-Explorer
  • Course Background: Built as a data science final project following Preceptor’s Primer for Bayesian Data Science using R, the tidyverse, Quarto, and GitHub Pages.
